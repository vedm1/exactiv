# Engagement approval process (evidence for the claim "No change without human approval")

Status: version 1, approved by the owner on 19 Nov 2026 (stated in chat). The claim `human-approval` is `ready`.

1. **Stages.** Every engagement is agreed in writing as stages (assess, pilot, modernise) with a deliverable for each stage.
2. **Named approver.** The client names one person who approves. We record the name in the scope document.
3. **No change without approval.** We do not change client code, client systems or a client release, and we do not
   release a deliverable, until the named approver has approved the stage in writing (email or signed document).
4. **Next stage.** The next stage starts only after the approval of the stage before it.
5. **Record.** We keep each approval with the engagement records for the length of the contract.
6. **AI output.** A person on our team reads AI-assisted output before we hand it over.
7. **Stop.** If the approver does not approve, we stop and do not continue by default.
