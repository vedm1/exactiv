A brand that cites itself.1

Exaktiv sells one idea: no value without a source. So the brand does the same. Every claim on the site carries a footnote. Every colour has one job. Every page reads like a document a reviewer can check.

  1. 1PRD-W, section 6, the claims register. A claim goes on the site only when its evidence exists.

The footnote is the logo in action: a value, a leader line, and the file it came from.

Three rules hold the whole system.

Every claim carries a source.

Numbers, promises and pillars get a cobalt footnote that leads to evidence. No footnote, no claim.

Colour has a job.

Cobalt means source. Vermilion means act. Marker means evidence. Nothing is decoration.

Headings do the work.

No kickers, tags or numbers above a title. No monospace. Hard rules and square corners hold the page.

The Leader mark.

A file, with one line cut out of it. The line leaves the file as a leader and ends at a vermilion dot: the finding. The file is an ink square. The line is a cut at line height. The finding is the only warm colour in the mark. The wordmark is Young Serif, outlined in every logo file, so it never depends on an installed font.

Primary on Paper. The default everywhere.

Reversed on Ink or Night. Footer and dark mode.

On Cobalt. Security pack covers only.

Stacked. Square formats and social avatars.

Mark only. When the name is already on the page.

Favicon and app icon. Below 24 px drop the leader; below 16 px drop the line. The dot stays.

Keep two dots of clear space on all sides. The lockup is never narrower than 96 px on screen or 22 mm in print.

The Trace: claim, number, source.

AI processing stays in your region.2

  1. 2Region register of model endpoints, security pack, part 3.
  1. The evidence band (.ex-mark) sits behind the exact words the evidence backs, never the whole sentence.
  2. The number (.ex-ref) is a cobalt superscript in Instrument Sans, counted per page, and links to its citation.
  3. The citation (.ex-cites) sits under a cobalt rule in Gelasio italic. It links to the evidence, or names where a reviewer gets it.
  4. The gate. A claim gets a number only when its claims-register status is "Ready". No evidence, no number, no claim.

Tokens in three layers.

Raw values (--ex-raw-*) feed role tokens named by job (--ex-source, --ex-act, --ex-evidence). Components read role tokens only, and dark mode swaps roles, never raw values. tokens/tokens.css is the source of truth; build_tokens.py generates tokens.json (W3C design tokens format) and tokens.ts.

Space runs on a 4 px base (4 to 96). Rules are 1.5 px, accent edges 6 px, focus rings 3 px with a 3 px gap. Radius is 0. No shadows, no blur, no gradients except the evidence band. Maximum width 1280 px, gutter 24 px, 68 characters per body line, transitions 120 ms.

Mobile first. Fluid between four breakpoints.

Spacing and text sizes grow smoothly from 320 px to 1440 px wide with clamp(), so nothing jumps. Layout changes only at the four breakpoints. CSS cannot read a variable inside @media, so a media query writes the breakpoint as a number; check_brand.py fails on any width that is not a breakpoint token.
